## 1. Principles of Regulatory Versioning

In academic regulations, two rules that specify different values or procedures do not automatically constitute an error or a mutual annihilation. Their validity depends on:
1. **Effective Date and Academic Council Ratification.**
2. **Program and School Jurisdiction** (e.g., University-wide vs. SCSET B.Tech).
3. **Cohort Applicability** (Grandfathering vs. Retroactive Application).

## 2. Detailed Regulatory Audits

### 2.1 Attendance Threshold: 70% vs. 75%
- **Rule A (Historical):** `SEAS-Ordinance-Rules-Regulations-2017.pdf`, Section IV, Clause 30:
  - *Text:* Minimum attendance requirement of 70% in each registered course.
  - *Applicability:* AY 2017-18 founding cohort.
- **Rule B (Current):** `Notification-Minimum-Attendance.pdf`, Ref No: BU/RO/Notification/307/2022:
  - *Text:* Minimum attendance requirement of 75% for End Semester Examination eligibility. Reviewed in the 13th Academic Council Meeting (29 July 2022) in consonance with UGC Regulations 2003.
  - *Applicability:* AY 2022-23 (odd semester onwards) across all undergraduate and B.Tech students.
- **Reconciliation & System Handling:**
  - The 2022 notification explicitly states its effective date ("with effect from Academic Year 2022-23").
  - The system retains both passages in the corpus.
  - When a query asks generically *"What is the minimum attendance requirement?"* without specifying cohort or year, the Evidence Controller detects a temporal version conflict (`CONFLICTING_VERSIONS`) and discloses both the 70% historical baseline and the current 75% mandate.
