## 5. Dataset Incompatibility & Separation Invariants

To avoid methodological contamination during R6 evaluation:
1. **Never Merge M6 and M9 Directly:** M6 is an English-only, 60-query set with 20 unanswerable queries; M9 consists of 60 semantic intents expanded into 4 language variants (240 total queries). They have different schemas and must be evaluated as separate benchmarks.
2. **Never Treat Language Variants as Independent Intent Samples:** The 240 queries in M9 represent only 60 independent intents. Reporting accuracy over 240 queries without intent-level clustering falsely inflates sample size and violates statistical independence assumptions. Grouped analysis by `intent_id` is mandatory.
3. **Never Label Synthetic Provisional Queries as Human Verified:** All 240 queries in M9 and all 60 queries in M6 are marked provisional/pending. They must be explicitly reported as automatically generated or auto-validated.
4. **Preserve Frozen Hashes:** Any script modifying `backend/data/dataset/` or `backend/data/experiments/m10/` is prohibited.
